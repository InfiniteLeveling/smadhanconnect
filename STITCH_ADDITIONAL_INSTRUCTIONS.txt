--- BEGIN STITCH INSTRUCTIONS ---

You are designing the frontend UI for "Samadhan Connect", India's premier national civic innovation and problem-solving ecosystem. The platform brings together five core stakeholders:
1. Everyday Citizens (reporting local civic problems with GPS photos & video evidence)
2. Students & Innovators (submitting technical prototypes and solving national challenges)
3. University R&D Labs & Faculty Mentors (academic endorsement and lab verification)
4. Government Ministries & Nodal Officers (posting national RFPs and managing district grievances)
5. Industry Partners & CSR Funds (funding student prototypes with milestone grants)

AESTHETIC ARCHETYPE & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:
- Modern Civic-Tech & GovTech SaaS: Strike the exact balance between institutional credibility (authoritative government trust) and tier-1 futuristic SaaS polish (inspired by Linear, Stripe, Apple, and Vercel).
- Visual Style: Clean, high-contrast, luminous, and kinetic. Use frosted glassmorphic surfaces, hairline borders, radial gradient mesh backgrounds, glowing status indicators, and deliberate micro-interactions.
- Never use generic placeholders like "Lorem Ipsum". Always use realistic Indian civic, governance, and academic context (e.g., Ministry of Jal Shakti, NDMC Delhi, IIT Madras, rural solar micro-grids, groundwater contamination).

1. COLOR SYSTEM & DESIGN TOKENS (STRICTLY ENFORCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Apply a strict 60-30-10 color harmony:
- 60% Dominant Base:
  * Light Mode: Crisp Slate-50 (#F8FAFC) background with pure white (#FFFFFF) elevated surfaces.
  * Dark Mode: Deep obsidian (#0B0F19) background with dark slate (#0F172A) elevated surfaces.
- 30% Structural Hierarchy:
  * Glass surfaces: Frosted glass (backdrop-blur-md bg-white/85 dark:bg-slate-900/85).
  * Borders: Hairline 1px borders (border-slate-200/80 dark:border-slate-800/80).
- 10% High-Impact Accent (Brand Emerald / Green):
  * brand-50: #F0FDF4 (subtle active pill background)
  * brand-100: #DCFCE7 (soft badge background)
  * brand-200: #BBF7D0 (hover border tint)
  * brand-300: #86EFAC (focus rings)
  * brand-400: #4ADE80 (radiant accents & glowing indicators)
  * brand-500: #22C55E (primary interactive highlights & success states)
  * brand-600: #16A34A (PRIMARY BRAND COLOR for buttons & key CTAs)
  * brand-700: #15803D (hover states for buttons)
  * brand-950: #052E16 (deep card gradients & dark accents)

Semantic Stakeholder & Status Colors:
- Government Ministries & Nodal Officers: Royal Blue (#2563EB)

- Academic Research & Universities: Deep Indigo (#4F46E5)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TYPOGRAPHY & TEXT HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Display Headings & Big Metric Numbers:
  * Font: 'Outfit', sans-serif
  * Weights: Bold (700) or ExtraBold (800)
  * Tracking: tracking-tight (-0.02em to -0.03em)
  * Use gradient text highlights on key hero phrases:
    `bg-gradient-to-r from-emerald-600 via-teal-500 to-green-600 bg-clip-text text-transparent`
- Body Text, Descriptions & Navigation:
  * Font: 'Plus Jakarta Sans' or 'Inter', sans-serif
  * Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600)
  * High legibility line-height (leading-relaxed for paragraphs)
- Metadata, Ticket Numbers & Currency:
  * Font: Monospace font (JetBrains Mono / tabular-nums) for Ticket IDs (e.g., #SAM-2026-8942), GPS coordinates (28.6139° N, 77.2090° E), and Grant Amounts (₹25,00,000).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ELEVATION, RADIUS & MICRO-INTERACTION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Border Radii:
  * Tags, pills & badges: rounded-full (9999px)
  * Inputs, buttons & small components: rounded-xl (12px)
  * Standard cards & containers: rounded-2xl (16px)
  * Hero containers & modal dialogs: rounded-3xl (24px)
- Shadows & Ambient Lighting:
  * Soft Ambient: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`
  * Radiant Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.25);`
- Micro-Interactions on Every Interactive Element:
  * All buttons: Include active press state `active:scale-[0.97] transition-transform duration-150`.
  * All cards: Include hover elevation `hover:-translate-y-1 hover:border-emerald-400 hover:shadow-xl transition-all duration-300`.
  * Status badges: Include a pulsating colored dot (`animate-pulse` or `animate-ping`) next to text.
  * Focus indicators: Always provide accessible `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-emerald-500 focus-visible:ring-offset-2`.
