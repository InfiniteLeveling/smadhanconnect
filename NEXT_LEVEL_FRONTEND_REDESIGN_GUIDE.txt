1. EXECUTIVE DESIGN VISION & CORE AESTHETIC PRINCIPLES
================================================================================
Samadhan Connect is not just another administrative portal; it is India's 
bridge between government challenges, academic R&D, industry grants, and student 
innovators. The design must communicate:
- TRUST & AUTHORITY: Institutional credibility (Govt/Universities).
- CUTTING-EDGE INNOVATION: Futuristic, high-tech, AI-empowered feel.
- ACCESSIBILITY & SPEED: Fluid, responsive, zero-clutter citizen reporting.

Core Aesthetic Pillars:
1. DEPTH & AMBIENT LIGHTING:
   - Ditch flat solid backgrounds. Use subtle radial gradient mesh backgrounds,
     faint dot-grid or grid-line overlays, and ambient glows (brand emerald/teal).
2. GLASSMORPHISM & BORDER HIGHLIGHTS:
   - High-end cards use frosted glass (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/70` in light mode,
     `bg-slate-900/80` in dark mode) paired with hairline borders:
     `border border-slate-200/80` with hover border transitions `hover:border-emerald-400`.
3. MICRO-INTERACTIONS & KINETIC FEEDBACK:
   - Every card should have subtle hover lifts (`-translate-y-1`), glow shadows,
     and magnetic button click states (`active:scale-95`).
4. DYNAMIC DATA & SOCIAL PROOF:
   - Live challenge counters, pulsating status badges, interactive impact charts,
     and real-time activity marquees make the platform feel ALIVE.

3. HOW TO MASTER GOOGLE STITCH & AI UI GENERATORS (EXACT WORKFLOW)
================================================================================
Follow this systematic 4-step pipeline when redesigning any page using Stitch or v0:

STEP 1: CAPTURE & SCREENSHOT THE EXISTING COMPONENT
- Open your running app at http://localhost:5173.
- Take a clean screenshot of the component you want to overhaul (e.g., ChallengeCard,
  Hero Section, or Problem Submission Form).

STEP 2: FEED INTO STITCH / V0 WITH CONTEXT
- In Stitch / v0, upload the screenshot or paste the existing JSX.
- Attach your design tokens:
  * Primary color: Emerald/Green (`#16a34a`, `#22c55e`)
  * Neutral background: Slate-50 / Slate-900
  * Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans (body), Outfit (display headings)
  * Aesthetic: "Modern Linear-style SaaS with glassmorphic cards, subtle borders,
    accessible contrast, and soft ambient glow."

STEP 3: PROMPT FOR SPECIFIC INTERACTIONS
- Prompt example:
  "Redesign this Challenge Card to look like a high-end fintech/civic-tech card.
   Add a live prize pill with gradient border, department logo with subtle ring,
   a progress bar for funding/timeline, tag chips with rounded-full styling,
   and a hover effect that reveals a 'View Challenge' button with smooth arrow translation."

STEP 4: EXPORT & INTEGRATE CLEANLY INTO VITE
- Export the React + Tailwind code.
- Ensure icons use `lucide-react` (which is already installed in package.json).
- Replace any hardcoded colors with your `brand-*` tailwind classes.

6. COLOR SYSTEM, TYPOGRAPHY & DESIGN TOKENS
================================================================================
Ensure your design tokens are strictly followed for maximum visual harmony:

1. COLOR PALETTE:
   - Dominant Background (60%):
     Light: `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) | Dark: `#0B0F19` (Deep Obsidian)
   - Card Surface (30%):
     Light: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with hairline `border-slate-200`
     Dark: `#111827` (Slate-900) with hairline `border-slate-800`
   - Accent & Brand (10%):
     Brand Emerald Primary: `#16A34A` (Tailwind `brand-600`)
     Brand Mint Glow: `#22C55E` / `#4ADE80` (Tailwind `brand-400` / `brand-500`)
     Urgent/Alert: `#EF4444` (Rose-500)
     Info/Govt: `#3B82F6` (Blue-500)

2. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY:
   - Display Headings (Hero, Section Titles):
     Font: 'Outfit', sans-serif | Weights: 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold)
     Tracking: `tracking-tight` (-0.02em)
   - Body & Interface Elements:
     Font: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif | Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold)
   - Code & Data Metrics:
     Font: 'JetBrains Mono' or 'Fira Code' for IDs, stats, and coordinates.

3. ELEVATION & SHADOW TOKENS (in index.css):
   - Soft Ambient: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
   - Emerald Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.25);`
   - Floating Card: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07);`
